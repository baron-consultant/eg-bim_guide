- Creates multiline text and lets you apply a variety of formatting.

![][image1]

## **1\. Creating Multiline Text** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/V0mAtgHFsFI)

1\) Run the "MTEXT" or "MT" command.  
2\) On screen, specify the area (rectangular window) in which to enter text.

- First point: the upper-left corner of the text box
- Second point: the lower-right corner of the text box

3\) Enter text in the text editor that appears within the specified area.  
4\) After finishing entering text, press the ESC key or Ctrl \+ Enter to exit.

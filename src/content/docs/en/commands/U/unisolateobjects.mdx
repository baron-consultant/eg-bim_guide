---
title: UNISOLATEOBJECTS
---

[image1]: ../../../../../assets/images/en/commands/unisolateobjects/image1.png

- Restores (redisplays) objects that were hidden using the `HIDEOBJECTS` or `ISOLATEOBJECTS` command.

![][image1]

## **1\. Restoring Hidden Objects** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/5DaArWtJ6Aw)

1\) Run the "UNISOLATEOBJECTS" command.  
2\) Press ENTER or the SPACE BAR to end the command.  
3\) All hidden objects in the drawing are restored and displayed again.

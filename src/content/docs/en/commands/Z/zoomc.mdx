- Sets a specified point as the center of the screen and zooms in or out according to the entered width value.

![][image1]

## **1\. Zooming Based on a Center Point** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/uyln7-TB6fg)

1\) Type "ZOOMC" in the Command Input Window.  
2\) Press the ENTER key.  
3\) Click the point you want to set as the center on screen.  
4\) Enter the width to display as a number, or in a scale format such as 2X, 0.5X, etc.  
5\) Press the ENTER key to apply the zoom based on the specified center point.  
![][image2]

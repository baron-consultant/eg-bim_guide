- Marks or inserts rebar on the drawing.

![][image1]

## **1\. Marking Rebar** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/RvI7btJUmnc)

1\) Type "PMARK" in the Command Line.  
2\) Click the point at which to specify the rebar location.  
3\) Click the leader line endpoint (the mark location).  
4\) The rebar mark and its text information are automatically inserted into the drawing.

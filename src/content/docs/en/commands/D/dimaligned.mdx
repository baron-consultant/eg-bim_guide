[image1]: ../../../../../assets/images/en/commands/dimaligned/image1.png

* Measures the sloped (diagonal) distance between two points and creates a dimension line aligned to that direction.

![][image1]

## **1\. Measuring a Diagonal** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/mmh0MJOnqEY)

1\) Type "DIMALIGNED" in the Command Line.  
2\) Click to specify the first point.  
3\) Click to specify the second point.  
4\) Specify the location of the dimension line.  
5\) A dimension line is created in the direction connecting the two selected points.

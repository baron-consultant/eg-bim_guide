[image1]: ../../../../../assets/images/en/commands/dimarc/image1.png

* Automatically calculates the length of an arc and creates a dimension for it.

![][image1]

## **1\. Measuring an Arc Length** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/ep1lbs5DyZo)

1\) Type "DIMARC" in the Command Line.  
2\) Click the arc to dimension.  
3\) Once selected, the actual length of the arc is displayed.

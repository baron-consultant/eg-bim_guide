title: DIMLINEAR
---

[image1]: ../../../../../assets/images/en/commands/dimlinear/image1.png

* Measures the horizontal or vertical distance (orthogonal distance) between two points and creates a dimension.

![][image1]

## **1\. Measuring a Distance** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/vObEqUI-Vrw)

1\) Type "DIMLINEAR" in the Command Line.  
2\) Click the first point.  
3\) Click the second point.  
4\) Click to specify the location of the dimension line.  
5\) The dimension line is automatically created in the horizontal or vertical direction.

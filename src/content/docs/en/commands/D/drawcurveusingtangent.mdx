title: DRAWCURVEUSINGTANGENT
---

[image1]: ../../../../../assets/images/en/commands/drawcurveusingtangent/image1.png

- Creates a circle or arc tangent to two selected lines.
- The lines used as tangents must not be parallel.

![][image1]

## **1\. Drawing a Circle or Arc Tangent to Two Lines** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/FARbpJeOPY8)

1\) Run the "DRAWCURVEUSINGTANGENT" command.  
2\) Select the shape to draw and click OK.  
3\) Select the two lines to use as tangents.  
4\) Click the location where you want to draw the shape; the shape is created.

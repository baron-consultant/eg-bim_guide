- Inserts a point rebar at the intersection point in the drawing.

![][image1]

## **1\. Insert a Point Rebar** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/pXNbKbrY6CQ)

1\) Type "INTREBAR" in the Command Line.  
2\) Click the two lines or objects that have an intersection point.  
3\) A point rebar symbol is automatically inserted at the intersection point.  
![][image2]

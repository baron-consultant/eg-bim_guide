title: RECTANGLE
---

[image1]: ../../../../../assets/images/en/commands/rectangle/image1.png

- Creates a rectangle as a polyline.
- You can draw a rectangle by setting property values.

![][image1]

- Setting option keywords for RECTANGLE

* Chamfer: Cuts the corners of the rectangle diagonally by the specified distance.
* Elevation: Specifies the Z-axis elevation from the XY plane at which to draw.
* Fillet: Rounds the corners of the rectangle by the specified radius value.
* Rotated: Creates the rectangle rotated by a specific angle.
* Square: Used to draw a square.
* Thickness: Creates the rectangle with thickness applied in the Z-axis direction.
* Width: Creates the rectangle with a specific line width.

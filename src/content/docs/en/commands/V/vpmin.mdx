title: VPMIN

[image1]: ../../../../../assets/images/en/commands/vpmin/image1.png

- Restores a currently maximized viewport to its original paper space size.

![][image1]

## **1\. Restoring a Viewport** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/ZY4IjKixYWs)

1\) Go to the Layout tab.  
2\) Maximize the viewport using the "VPMAX" command.  
2\) Type "VPMIN" in the Command Line.  
3\) Press the ENTER key.  
4\) The maximized viewport is restored to its original Layout space size.

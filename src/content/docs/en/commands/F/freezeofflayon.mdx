---
title: FREEZEOFFLAYON
---

[image1]: ../../../../../assets/images/en/commands/freezeofflayon/image1.png

- A command that simultaneously reverses the 'frozen' and 'on' state of all layers in the current drawing. That is, layers that were thawed become frozen, layers that were frozen become thawed, and at the same time, layers that were on are turned off, and layers that were off are turned on.

![][image1]

## **1\. Reversing the Frozen State** [**Demo Video**](https://youtu.be/c4FsTlC_HOQ)

1\) Type the "FREEZEOFFLAYON" command in the Command Line.  
2\) Press the ENTER key.  
3\) Layers that are currently on are turned off, and layers that were off are turned on.  
4\) At the same time, layers that were frozen are thawed, and layers that were thawed are frozen.  
5\) As a result, the display state of the drawing is completely reversed — objects that were previously hidden become visible, and objects that were visible disappear.
